Dduke plays audio files already stored on your device. It doesn't have accounts, doesn't require sign-in, and doesn't upload your music library anywhere. This page explains, plainly, what the app touches and why.

02

Permissions the app requests

Each permission below exists for one specific, on-screen feature. None of them are used to collect data for us, they let the app read files or show controls on your own device.

PermissionWhat it's for
READ_MEDIA_AUDIOLets the app list the audio files already on your device (Android 13+), so it can build your song and folder list.
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGEThe same purpose as above, for Android 12 and earlier.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACKKeeps music playing when you switch apps or lock your screen, with a visible playback notification.
POST_NOTIFICATIONSShows the "now playing" notification with play/pause/skip controls.
WRITE_SETTINGSOnly used the moment you tap "Set as Ringtone" on a song, it's what lets Android change your ringtone. Never used otherwise.
03

What's stored, and where

Your favorites, playlists, recently-played history, per-song play counts, and the "exclude WhatsApp audio" preference are saved in the app's private local storage on your device (Android DataStore). None of it is transmitted to us or to any server, we don't operate one. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it immediately.

How the WhatsApp audio filter works

If you enable it, the app checks the folder name of each audio file it already has permission to see (for example, one literally named WhatsApp Voice Notes or WhatsApp Audio) and leaves matching files out of your library view. That check happens entirely on your device against file and folder names it can already read, nothing about your files or folders is sent anywhere to make this decision.

04

Advertising

Dduke shows banner ads through Google's AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to keep the app free. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect an advertising identifier and general device/app information under Google's own privacy practices. We don't receive your listening history, your files, or your identity from this, only Google's ad platform handles that data, per its own terms.

You can review how Google handles this data, and manage ad personalization for your Google Account, at policies.google.com/privacy.

05

Third parties

The only third-party service integrated into this app is Google AdMob, described above. There's no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no other tracker built into the app.

06

Children

Dduke is not directed at children and isn't part of Google Play's Designed for Families program. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

07

Your choices

  • Revoke the audio/storage permission at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Dduke → Permissions; the app will simply show no songs until it's granted again.
  • Manage ad personalization for your device at Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Uninstalling the app removes every local preference described in Section 3.
08

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects or how it uses permissions changes, this page will be updated and the "last updated" date at the top will change accordingly.
